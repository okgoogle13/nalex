03

The pattern changes after Conflict

The record shows an even split before Aftermath. The difference appears later, when Naomi starts each of the 5 recorded sessions in that phase.

The record does not tell us why Naomi reached out, what she hoped would happen, or why Alex did not start a recorded session during that period.

04

What we can't know from this record

The record cannot establish:

  • what Naomi was feeling when she reached out.
  • whether the contact was wanted or unwelcome.
  • why Alex did not start a recorded session.
  • whether either person communicated outside the recorded material.
05

Reflection prompt

When one person is doing most of the reaching out, what would make the next exchange feel more balanced and clear?

06

A useful distinction

Reaching out can mean many different things:

  • wanting to repair the connection.
  • wanting an answer.
  • wanting to clarify what happened.
  • wanting to check whether contact is still possible.

The record shows who started the exchanges. It does not tell us which meaning applied.
