### Turning Point 1: The Intellectualization Fracture
**Phase / Session:** Conflict, June 26 (EIDs B030-B034)

- **Trigger:** 
  - Alex (B031): *"loneliness is something that I think will always be with you... you're definitely the sort of person who just Sort of gives it all and just allows themselves to sort of be treated like shit because they're lonely"*
  - Naomi (B032): *"I'm not acting out of loneliness and fear. You're incorrect there... I've always been alone. Always will be, always was, always will be"*
- **Defense & Anxiety (Lens 1):** Alex uses *Intellectualization/Projection*, and Naomi responds with *Defended Invulnerability*. **Hypothesized latent anxiety:** Dread of yielding conversational power or being "wrong" on the record. **Immediate relational effect:** Mutual refusal to engage the core emotional hurt, displacing the conflict into a rigid ideological standoff that blocks genuine connection.
- **Rupture/Repair Dynamics (Lens 2):** 
  - *Observable shifts:* The conversational structure abruptly shifts from a vulnerability exchange into a rigid standoff. Alex employs a massive bimodal turn length anomaly (110-second monologue at B031) which functionally overwhelms the pacing.
  - *Trajectory:* Alex attempts a structural repair by trying to downplay the conflict and proposing a hangout (B033), then immediately pivots to avoidance and topic-switching (B034). Left structurally unresolved.
- **Link to Canonical Findings:** Initiates the `dyad_mutual_non_answering` dynamic. Aligns with the `alex_bimodal_turn_length` metric and `alex_feeling_ambushed`.
- **Forward-Looking Evaluation:** To prevent future intellectualization fractures, both participants must practice identifying when a structural conversation shifts into a debate over objective "truth," and pivot instead toward validating the subjective emotional experience driving the need to be "right."

**Phase / Session:** Aftermath, July 11 (EIDs G042-G112)

- **Trigger:**

- **Rupture/Repair Dynamics (Lens 2):**

- **Link to Canonical Findings:** Dictates `alex_topic_contingent_availability`. Cements the structural asymmetry of the Aftermath phase, contextualizing `alex_zero_initiation` (0 of 5 Aftermath sessions opened by Alex).
- **Forward-Looking Evaluation:** Transitioning from "topic-contingent availability" to a structured, mutually agreed-upon "grief window" could allow one participant to safely process their feelings without the other feeling compelled to defend against a perceived ambush, easing the initiation asymmetry.
