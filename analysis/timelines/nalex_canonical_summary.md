## Structural patterns visible across the 16

Stated as observations, not diagnoses. Each is traceable to specific events.

1. **The retrieval deadlock recurs three times** — CONF-01 (B022/B023), CONF-02 (B088–B106), AFT-02 (G064/G032). Same shape each time: one party asks the other to name the thing first; neither does.
2. **The acknowledgement demand is mutual** — 4 Aftermath instances each. Neither party is only asking or only withholding.
3. **Naomi's voice-message timing account is stated four times** — B066, G182, G055, G194 — and is never engaged with on its merits. The timing record supports it.
4. **Exits are declared more often than held** — 2 of 11 Conflict-phase sign-offs held; B063 and B107 are both re-entries or breaches. Both parties continue after stating they are finished (Alex: B063, B107; Naomi: G108 → G067).
5. **One acknowledgement occurred and did not survive its session** — AFT-06 → AFT-07, 23 minutes.
6. **Warmth and conflict-adjacency are separable** — 129 warm minutes at AFT-11 collapse in under six once the dinner debt is raised.
7. **The corpus has no closure event** — it ends mid-protest, one-sided.
