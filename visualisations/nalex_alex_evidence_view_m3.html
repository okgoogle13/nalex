02

What the record does not establish

Named limits. These are not gaps to be filled by inference.

  • Motive and function are not in the data. The corpus counts sign-off turns. It does not establish why they occur, what they were intended to do, or how they were received. Any reading of them as self-regulation, as withdrawal, or as anything else is an interpretation the record cannot settle.
  • Aftermath opens and closes cannot be separated from availability. Alex opens 0 of 5 Aftermath sessions. The record does not distinguish a choice not to open from an absence of opportunity to open.
  • The Conflict thread cannot be integrity-checked. 77 events carry no sha256, and all 77 fall in the Conflict phase. The project's “always join on sha256” invariant cannot be applied there, so Conflict figures rest on a weaker join than Baseline or Aftermath.
  • Silence is unmeasured, not empty. The window 2026-07-06 to 2026-07-10 carries no speaker rows at all. Nothing about either person's conduct in that window can be read off this page.
03

One possible reflection

Not a finding, not a recommendation. One way of holding the above.

Reflection · not evidence

The record shows sign-offs and closes concentrated on one side, and reply latency that varies least of any measure here. One possible reflection is how a closing turn that names a return point may read differently from one that does not — though the material does not establish how any closing turn was received.

This paragraph is not derived from the measurements above. It is one reading among others, and the evidence does not select between them.
