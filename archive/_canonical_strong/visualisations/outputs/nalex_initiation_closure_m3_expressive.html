About this view

What it shows. A single question: who opens and who closes sessions in each phase. Nothing else is layered in — every figure comes from one small set of records, and each one can be opened and read in full.

How to use it. Tap any tile to open the record behind it, along with a forward-looking direction to try. Use the bottom deck to switch between Opens and Closes, to narrow to a single phase, and to bring the Sign-offs layer over the top.

What it is for. This is a reflection surface, not a verdict. It describes what was recorded so you can experiment with a different move — it does not explain why anything happened, and it does not decide anything about what comes next.
