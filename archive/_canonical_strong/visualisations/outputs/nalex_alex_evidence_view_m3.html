03

The important change is timing

The clearest shift appears once Conflict begins. Before that point, the record does not show this pattern.

This does not tell us whether stepping away was helpful or harmful in each moment. It does show that ending the exchange became a more visible part of the interaction after Conflict started.

04

What we can't know from this record

The record cannot establish:

  • why Alex stepped away.
  • whether Alex intended to create distance.
  • whether a sign-off was understood as temporary or final.
  • what happened outside the recorded exchanges.
05

Reflection prompt

When a conversation needs to stop, what would make the pause feel clear rather than open-ended?

06

A useful distinction

There is a difference between:

“I need to stop here for now.”

and:

“This conversation is over.”

The record shows when clear endings appear. It does not decide which kind of ending was intended.
