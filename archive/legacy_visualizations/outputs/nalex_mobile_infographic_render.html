Actionable Interventions Protocol

For Naomi: Pacing & Structure

  • Write It Out First: Write 110+ word thoughts in a private doc. Distill to one feeling and one request before sending.
  • Match the Length: If Alex replies with 20 words, reply with max 40 words. If asymmetry exceeds 2:1, pause.
  • Stick to One Topic: Discuss only one incident at a time (e.g., June 26). Do not litigate the past.

For Alex: Engagement & Regulation

  • Structured Pause: Replace abrupt exits with a committed return time (e.g., "I need a 24-hour break, but I will return to this").
  • The "Truth" Trap: Pivot away from rigid intellectual debates over objective truth and validate the subjective emotional experience instead.
