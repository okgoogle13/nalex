# Pass B: Macro Participant Profile

## Context
Use this for whole-corpus or aggregated synthesis.
*Note: Always apply the `shared_system_prompt.md` before executing this pass.*

## Task
For each requested participant (Naomi and Alex), construct a high-level psychodynamic profile and feedback report using two sublenses:

### 1. Role & Mirroring Lens
- Summarize pronoun usage ("I/you/we") and average message length by phase.
- Note salient mirroring patterns (lexical convergence/divergence) and power asymmetries.
- Propose 1-2 hypothesized role dyads (e.g., "Explainer / Gatekeeper", "Pursuer / Constrained responder") with a brief definition, phase-dependence, and supporting evidence.

### 2. Constructive Feedback Lens
- **Structural Strengths**: 3-5 behavior-focused points supporting connection/clarity.
- **Structural Risks**: 3-5 behavior-focused points describing unintended friction, worded in softened language (e.g., "Tends to... which may be exhausting").
- **Unresolved Tensions**: 2-4 points describing internal risks or deadlocks that may erode their sense of safety/dignity if left unchanged.

## Output Format
For each participant, use the following structure:

## Participant: [Name]

### 1. Role & Mirroring
- **Pronoun & Mirroring Note**: [Summary of lexical stance and convergence/divergence across phases]
- **Role Hypotheses**: 
  - *[Role Dyad]*: [Definition] | *Evidence*: [Quotes/metrics] | *Phase*: [When it's strongest]

### 2. Constructive Feedback Profile
- **Structural Strengths**:
  - [Strength 1] (Evidence: ...)
  - [Strength 2] (Evidence: ...)
- **Structural Risks**:
  - [Risk 1] (Evidence: ...)
  - [Risk 2] (Evidence: ...)
- **Unresolved Tensions**:
  - [Tension 1] 
  - [Tension 2] 
- **Forward-Looking Evaluation**:
  - [Constructive, behavior-focused recommendation synthesizing a path forward for this specific participant's structural patterns]

*Constraint: Ensure all claims are explicitly anchored in phase-level metrics, quotes, or qualitative annotations.*
